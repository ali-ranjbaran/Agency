# Intake questions

Full interview, with follow-ups to ask when the first answer is too
generic to build from. If `onboard-client` already ran and captured
some of this, confirm rather than re-ask from scratch — but confirm out
loud, don't silently assume the stored answer is still current.

## Industry & services

- What exactly do you do? (Get the specific service list, not just the
  trade category — "HVAC" isn't enough; "furnace repair, install, duct
  cleaning, AC service" is.)
- Follow-up if vague: which of these makes you the most money per job?
  Which do you actually want more of?

## Service area

- Which cities/towns/regions do you actually serve?
- Follow-up: is there anywhere just outside that range you'd still take
  a job if asked? (Sometimes worth a mention, sometimes not — ask rather
  than guessing.)

## Primary conversion action (gate — see SKILL.md step 2)

- If someone lands on this site ready to hire you right now, what do you
  want them to do? Pick one: call, fill a form, book online, request a
  quote.
- Follow-up if they say "all of them": which one, if it's the only thing
  that happens today, would you be happiest with?

## Brand vibe & assets

- Three words for how you want the business to come across.
- Do you have a logo? Brand colors? Existing photos you have rights to
  use?
- Follow-up: for any existing photos — do you have documented permission
  to use them, or are they yours/taken by you? (See hard rule in
  SKILL.md — don't use client-supplied photography without confirming
  this.)

## Competitors

- Name 2–3 competitors.
- What do you think they do better than you, if anything?
- Follow-up: what do you do better than them that customers don't
  usually find out until they've already hired you? (Often becomes the
  "why us" section.)

## Tone

- If a customer described your business in one sentence to a friend,
  what would you want them to say?

## Must-have pages

- Beyond the homepage, what do you need? (Common: services, service
  area/locations, about, reviews/testimonials, contact.)
- Follow-up: anything a competitor's site has that you specifically want
  or specifically don't want?

## The fear question (the one that matters most)

- What is the thing your customers are most worried about before they
  call someone in your trade? (Common answers: being overcharged, a bad
  job that has to be redone, hiring someone unlicensed, getting ghosted
  after booking, safety in their home.)
- Follow-up if they say "I don't know": ask what the most common
  objection or hesitation is when they're on the phone with a new
  customer. That hesitation is usually the fear.
