- "{QUOTE_ABOUT_CALLS}"

## 2 · What it costs you now

{MISSED_PER_MONTH} missed calls a month × {CLOSE_RATE} of callers who would have booked ×
{AVG_JOB_VALUE} average job = **{MONTHLY_COST_OF_MISSED} a month** in jobs that went to

All three inputs are your numbers. The day-25 report replaces them with measured ones.

## 3 · What we'll do

- Every missed call gets a text from your number, with your name, in about 10 seconds.
- Every finished job gets one review request, and at most one reminder.
- Customers from the last two years get a tune-up text before the cold.

## 4 · What you get in 30 days, and how we prove it

On day 25 you get a one-page report: calls, missed calls, texts sent, replies, jobs you
confirm were booked, reviews. Every number is labelled measured, confirmed by you, or
estimated. Spam calls are counted separately and left out.

## 5 · The work you don't see

- Every message checked against Canada's anti-spam rules: who it can go to, for how long,
  with STOP on every text.
- The text-back tested on your carrier, from 2 carriers, before it goes live.
- Your Google Business Profile checked and fixed: hours, categories, service area.
- {IF_SITE} The site passes our launch check before it goes live: forms tested into your
  CRM, accessibility, speed score 90+, call tracking seen working.

## 6 · The number

**{PRICE}** — {WHAT_IT_COVERS}. Tool costs included. Paid upfront; the monthly part is
charged at the start of each month.

## 7 · If it doesn't work

If the day-25 report doesn't show it's working, you keep the site and we switch the rest
off. No retainer.
