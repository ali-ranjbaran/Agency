# Scope statement

> Attached to an SOW. It lists what the fee covers, what it doesn't, and how changes are
> handled. Not legal advice.

Client: {CLIENT_TRADING_NAME} · SOW: {SOW_NUMBER}

## Included

**Entry (website)**
- Up to {PAGE_COUNT} pages, written from a 30-minute call with the owner
- Quote/contact form into the Client's inbox and CRM, tested end to end
- Click-to-call, Google Analytics, call tracking, verified live
- Privacy policy and cookie consent
- Mobile layout checked at 375, 768 and 1280 pixels wide
- 2 revision rounds
- 30 days of the four core automations, free

**Core (retainer)**
- CRM sub-account set up and maintained
- Missed-call text-back, review request, quote follow-up, past-customer reactivation,
  emergency reply
- Carrier forwarding tested on the Client's carrier
- Monthly one-page report
- Wording changes to existing automations, up to {CORE_CHANGES_PER_MONTH} per month

## Not included

- Photography, video, logo design
- Copywriting beyond the pages listed
- Paid ads (Google, Meta) and their spend
- Answering calls, or listening to them
- Call recording (separate opt-in change, with its own disclosure)
- Integrations not named in the SOW (for example Jobber, Housecall Pro sync)
- Migrating more than {MIGRATION_CONTACTS} existing contacts
- Work caused by the Client changing carrier, phone system or domain registrar without
  telling Arkosa

## How changes are handled

1. The Client sends the change in writing (email is fine).
2. Arkosa replies within {CHANGE_REPLY_DAYS} business days: in scope (done in the next
   change window) or a change order (hours × {CHANGE_ORDER_RATE}, and the date it would be
   done).
3. Work on a change order starts after the Client approves it in writing and pays.
4. A change order never changes the consent rules in `casl-wording.md`.
