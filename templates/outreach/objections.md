# Objection handling

> **DRAFT v0, Illia rewrites by hand before use.** Plan v4 §3.5 and §10.1. Open the
> conversion call with the report, not the price. Ask "What surprised you?" Then "Keep it
> running?"

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We use Jobber / Housecall Pro, it already texts." | "Keep it. It's where your jobs live. This covers the calls that never become a job in Jobber, because nobody answered them. Your report shows {N} of those." |
| "Too expensive." | Point to the recovered-revenue line. If that line is under the price, don't argue: offer a shorter commitment, or let it switch off. |
| "I'll just call people back myself." | "You did, for {N} of the {M} missed calls. The report shows the gap." |
| "Texting customers feels spammy." | "It only replies to people who called you or bought from you, inside the legal windows, with STOP on every message." |
| "My nephew does my website." | "Keep him on the site. I'm not replacing it. This runs underneath your phone number." |
| "We're slammed, no time." | "That's when missed calls are highest. Setup takes you 30 minutes." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Sure. The automations switch off on day 31 unless you say go. Want me to hold them until Friday?" |
| "What if you disappear?" | "Number porting and data export on exit are in the contract. I'm local and you get a report every month." |
| "I have Wix / Squarespace." | "Keep it. This runs under your phone number, not your website. Wix can sell you an app; you'd still be the one setting it up and checking it. I set it up, test it on your carrier, and show you every month what it caught." |

## Rules

- Never argue a number the report doesn't support.
- Never compete on monthly price with a website builder (plan §10.1). Talk about the cost
  of missed jobs, in their numbers.
- If they say no twice for the same reason, log the reason. Three of the same reason is a
  signal for the §2.6 decision tree.
