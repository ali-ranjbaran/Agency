# Cold call opener (30 seconds)

> **DRAFT v0, Illia rewrites by hand before use.** Plan v4 §2.3. Calls fall under the CRTC
> Unsolicited Telecommunications Rules, not CASL. Before the first dial: check the internal
> do-not-call list, calling hours, and call from the business line, not a personal mobile.

1. "Hi, it's Illia from Arkosa here in Saskatoon. This is a cold call. Can I have 30
   seconds, and then you can hang up on me?"
2. "When your guys are on a job and the phone rings, who picks it up?"
3. Let them answer. Most owners say "me", "my wife" or "voicemail". Write down which.
4. "That's the only thing I fix. Missed calls get a text back in seconds, so the customer
   waits for you instead of calling the next company."
5. Go to `demo-pitch.md`.

## If they say

- **"Not interested."** "Fair enough. Want me to take you off my list?" If yes, add them to
  the internal do-not-call list before the next dial.
- **"Send me something."** "Sure. What's the best email?" Only send to an address they gave
  you on the call, and log that they asked.
- **"Who gave you this number?"** "It's the number on your website / Google listing."
- **Voicemail.** Don't leave a pitch. "Hi, Illia from Arkosa in Saskatoon, about missed
  calls. I'll send a short email." Then log it.

## Log after every call

Date, time, outcome (talked / voicemail / no answer / do-not-call), who answers their phone,
next step. Logged in `admin/metrics.md` counts and the console, not in this file.
