# Demo-number pitch

> **DRAFT v0, Illia rewrites by hand before use.** Plan v4 §2.3. Never quote a price before
> the call.

1. "Don't take my word for it. Call {DEMO_NUMBER} right now, let it ring, and hang up."
   Stay on the line, or say you'll call back in two minutes.
2. When the text arrives: "That goes out from your number, with your name, 24/7."
3. "Plus a review request after every job, and a tune-up text to everyone you've served in
   the last two years."
4. "Your own phones still ring first. Nothing changes unless nobody picks up."
5. Close for the next step, not the sale: "Can I get 20 minutes Thursday to show you what
   it'd look like with your numbers?"

## Before the 20 minutes, ask

- Roughly how many calls a week, and how many go to voicemail?
- What's an average job worth? A new install?
- What do you use for jobs today (Jobber, Housecall Pro, paper)?

These go into the proposal's "what it costs them now" line, in their words.

## If the demo text doesn't arrive

Say so: "That should have arrived in 10 seconds. I'll check what happened and call you
back." Then check the demo-line alerts before the next dial.
